How we work together

A clear process. Your approval at every creative milestone.

01

Share your brief

Start with a short project enquiry. Once we confirm the fit and scope together, we guide you through the detailed creative brief.

02

Choose one of five storylines

We develop five storyline options from your brief. You select one direction and approve it in writing. We document your selection before any further production work begins.

Approval gate: one selected storyline, confirmed in writing.
03

Approve the scenes & storyboard

We turn the selected storyline into detailed scenes and a storyboard. You review the sequence, dialogue and visual direction and give written approval before we move on.

Approval gate: scenes and storyboard, confirmed in writing.
04

Approve the visual references

We prepare clearly labelled character, product, packaging, outfit, brand-colour, location and prop references. You approve the exact image versions in writing before film generation begins.

Approval gate: production references, confirmed in writing.
05

We generate & edit your film

We produce the scenes from the approved direction and bring them together with editing and sound. The approved storyline, storyboard and references guide production.

06

One consolidated revision round

Review the finished-video draft and send your feedback together in one list. Every package includes one consolidated finished-video revision round within the agreed scope.

One round across all packages. Any new scope is discussed separately before additional work.
07

Final delivery & acknowledgment

We deliver the agreed files through a download link or your agreed channel. A completion email records the project name, files, final duration and formats, where to access them, the agreed revision completed and any remaining acceptance or payment step. You acknowledge receipt to close out the project.
