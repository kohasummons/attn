## Shared prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a companion variation of the supplied Attention University abstract background for another Attention Factory website card. Preserve its fine photographic grain, softly defocused frosted-glass light, translucent depth, restrained saturation and editorial simplicity. Change the palette and arrangement as specified below. Use two or three broad organic colour fields, subtle halation and smooth gradients. Brightest light in upper-middle/right, top-left quiet and dark enough for a small white label. Lower 40% smoothly dark and calm for white headline/description overlaid in code. Natural darkening with no separate panel. Full bleed, robust mobile crop. Background artwork only, no text, letters, logos, borders, watermarks, flowers, recognizable objects, circuitry, robots, glossy 3D objects, sharp geometry or harsh neon.
